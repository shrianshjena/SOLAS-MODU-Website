# SOLAS MODU Marine Services

The official website of SOLAS MODU Marine Services Pvt. Ltd., Mumbai, India.

**Live site:** [solasmodu.net](https://solasmodu.net)

## About

SOLAS MODU Marine Services delivers trusted marine and offshore services across the industry: marine surveys and certifications, advanced non-destructive testing, survival systems, ship and boat design and repair, broking and trading, and collaborative project support. The company serves ships, rigs, and offshore assets with a commitment to safety, compliance, and dependable delivery.

## The website

The site presents the company's six service divisions alongside its certifications, a gallery of field operations, career opportunities, and direct contact channels:

- Advanced NDT
- Survival Systems
- Ship & Boat Design
- Broking & Trading
- Collaboration
- Certificates

- Careers
